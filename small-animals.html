Small-animal owners

Build a comfortable, species-appropriate home

Small animals can be a good fit for some households, including homes with limited space or schedules. Housing needs differ substantially by species, so environment and enrichment matter.

Possible companions

Consider animals such as hermit crabs, mice, gerbils, hamsters, guinea pigs, or chinchillas. Depending on the animal, a comfortable environment may use a small or large cage or a glass aquarium.

Core housing supplies

  • Bedding or substrate
  • Toys and enrichment
  • An exercise wheel when appropriate
  • Water dish or drip bottle
  • Food dish
  • Grooming equipment

Social needs matter too

Consider whether your new pet needs a friend or will be content living alone. Species, individual temperament, and appropriate housing all affect this decision.

When choosing products:
Match the enclosure, bedding, enrichment, and accessories to the species rather than simply choosing whatever fits the available space.
