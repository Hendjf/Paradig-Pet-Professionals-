Fish owners

Healthy water starts with testing

Fish can live in fresh or saltwater depending on species. Whether you are bringing fish home for the first time or maintaining an established aquarium, the supplied guidance recommends testing aquarium water once per week.

Freshwater fish

Consult a Pexpert to help assess compatibility among species. Use a water-testing kit and monitor chemistry so fish can adapt to the aquarium environment.

General freshwater guidance
MeasureSupplied guideline
pH6.8–7.6
AmmoniaAny level above zero is harmful
Nitrates5–10 ppm
NitritesLess than 0.5 ppm

Saltwater fish

As with freshwater fish, consider compatibility and appropriate environmental conditions for the species you keep.

General saltwater guidance
MeasureSupplied guideline
Salinity1.020–1.028 sg
pH7.6–8.4
AmmoniaAny level above 0.1 ppm requires attention
Nitrates10–40 ppm
NitritesLess than 0.2 ppm
