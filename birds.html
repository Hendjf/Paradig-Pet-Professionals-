Bird owners

Companionship, enrichment & well-being

Birds range from the tiny Cuban bee hummingbird to the much larger ostrich. Every species and individual bird is unique, particularly when it comes to relational needs and well-being.

Watch for changes in behavior

The supplied guidance notes that birds can experience changes in mood and behavior. Possible contributing factors include illness, losing a companion, or becoming bored.

Some signs that should prompt closer attention include:

  • Reduced appetite
  • Increasing irritability
  • Aggressive behavior
  • Songs or vocalizations taking on a different, more solemn tone

Early attention matters

Tell your veterinarian if you notice concerning symptoms or meaningful changes in your bird's normal behavior, appetite, or activity.

Enrichment matters.
Use appropriate interaction, social contact, and mentally engaging activities that fit your bird's species and individual needs.
